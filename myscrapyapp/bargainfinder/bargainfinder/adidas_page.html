    adidas Women's Hoodies & Sweatshirts

    Women's hoodies and sweatshirts are a wardrobe staple, and you can find your favorite styles, iconic looks and fresh, forward-thinking designs when you explore the wide range of women's pullovers at adidas. Performance pullovers are designed to keep you cool and comfortable when you're on the go. Moisture-managing fabric and built-in stretch feel good through cool-weather training and outdoor activities that get your heart pumping. Women's crewnecks come in a variety of designs, including quarter-zip and full-zip styles that make for easy on-and-off when you're on the move. You can also choose from slim sweatshirts with a close fit or oversized women's hoodies and sweatshirts that make for comfortable layering.

    Women's sweaters offer cozy comfort whether you're out and about in the chill or relaxing at home on the couch. With construction ranging from stretchy knit and soft cotton to fleece and cozy French Terry, they offer different levels of warmth depending on which one you choose. Lightweight hoodies and sweatshirts for women are perfect for transitional weather and even warmer days when you just want a breathable layer over a tank. Heavyweight pullovers can ward off serious chill, with details like ribbed cuffs and hem and a drawcord-adjustable hood adding to the cozy feel. Find the styles that match your look you when you shop the collection of women's hoodies and sweatshirts from adidas.

    Women's Hoodies & Sweatshirts Frequently Asked Questions

    What's a performance hoodie?
    A performance hoodie is a hooded sweatshirt designed for working out. It’s ideal for cold gyms, winter workouts or any time it’s chilly. Wear a hoodie during warm-up exercises. The warmth helps your muscles loosen up to prevent injury or strain, helping your body perform better. Women’s hoodies may be a pullover style or have a front zip to help you adjust your temperature. Zip pockets provide a place for your phone or keys. Many women also enjoy the comfort and coverage a hoodie provides, allowing you to perform with fewer mental distractions.
    How do I clean my adidas ZNE hoodie?
    To clean your adidas Z.N.E. women’s hoodie, turn it inside-out and make sure all zips and fasteners are closed. Machine wash it with mild detergent and cold water. Tumble dry on the low heat setting. Feel free to touch it up with a cool iron if you want. Don’t use fabric softener or bleach, and don’t dry clean. Learn how to wash all types of jackets on the adidas blog.
    What is the softest material for hoodies?
    The softest material for hoodies is cotton fleece. Many women’s sweatshirts are made with cotton or a cotton-poly blend. But other materials, such as 100% polyester, are also soft and comfortable against the skin. A fleece lining will make a hoodie feel ultra-soft. And French terry fabric has a soft-pile inner layer for cozy comfort.
